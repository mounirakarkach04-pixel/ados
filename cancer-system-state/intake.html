05 · Conditional regulation map

EU AI Act: What matters?

A legal checklist, not automatic compliance. Applicability depends on intended purpose, AI functionality, product classification and role. A non-AI spreadsheet is not automatically an AI system.

Articles 6–15 · classification, risk, data, logs, transparency, oversight, accuracy ↗ 2026 amendment · high-risk dates: Annex III 2027 / Annex I products 2028 ↗ FDA CDS guidance · intended use and independent review ↗

Conditional AI Act articles: 4 (literacy), 6 (classification), 9 (risk), 10 (data governance), 11 (documentation), 12 (record keeping), 13 (instructions/transparency), 14 (human oversight), 15 (accuracy/robustness/security), 16 (provider obligations), 26 (deployer), 27 (fundamental-rights impact in specified cases), 43 (conformity), 49 (registration), 50 (transparency where applicable), 72 (post-market monitoring).
