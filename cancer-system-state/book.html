The thesis and its boundaries

What can systems thinking explain—and where must inference stop?

The book uses legitimate in a narrow descriptive sense: a state can be possible under declared model assumptions. This never means that cancer is beneficial, ethically acceptable, inevitable or beyond treatment.

Its publicly archived specialist research edition develops this distinction across 50 disease-specific conceptual profiles, together with a framework for questioning assumptions, provenance, contradictory evidence and the temptation to extrapolate from elegant mathematics to actual patients.

What the reader gains: a disciplined way to distinguish system-model hypotheses from established biomedical facts, reconstruct the evidence behind a research claim, challenge alternative explanations and record what remains unproven. These are research-literacy tools, not diagnostic pathways.

The reading-to-research pathway

A three-step approach to evidence-governed exploration

01Read and specify the claim

Choose a conceptual profile and distinguish what the book actually states from a new hypothesis.

02Experiment with a fictional model

Change toy-model parameters, inspect uncertainty and compare competing explanations—without patient inputs.

03Reverse-audit the inference

Check provenance and counterevidence; export a local research passport. Gate 2 remains BLOCK.

PIGA separates admission of a research inference (Gate 1) from authorization of a real-world action (Gate 2). A7SEM treats seven dimensions as interacting, not a mandatory clinical sequence. A7SEM Reverse checks claims backwards against their evidence and possible counterexamples. A finite QA loop means repeatable, bounded checks—not unsupervised endless computation.
