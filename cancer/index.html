07 · Research boundaries & sources

Not a medical device
Does the word “legitimate” mean cancer is good or healthy?

No. It denotes a state that is admissible within the conditions of a theoretical system model. It is not medical, moral or clinical approval.

What do the reported 44 vs 3 components demonstrate?

Only geometrical component counts under a segmentation/adjacency definition. Labels and case identities require independent confirmation. They do not establish 44 vs 3 verified tumors.

Can this app identify cancer, predict outcomes or guide therapy?

No. It accepts no patient information and offers no clinical actions. Its controls generate fictional or methodological demonstrations.

How do I use the downloaded file on Android?

Open this single HTML file using an app that supports local HTML. All internal navigation uses in-file section links. External scientific reference links require internet access. If Android reports content URI not found, save a fresh copy and open it through Files/My Files without moving it while open.
